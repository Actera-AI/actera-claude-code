# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, or any other project space, is expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent and ask
  before concluding.
- Be constructive. Give specific, actionable feedback and accept it in return.
- Be inclusive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized
  language or imagery, and publishing someone's private information are not
  tolerated.
- Stay on topic. Keep project spaces focused on the project.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that do not
follow these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently exclude anyone
who repeatedly or seriously violates them.

Report unacceptable behavior to [hello@actera.dev](mailto:hello@actera.dev).
Reports are handled confidentially. Security vulnerabilities go through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
